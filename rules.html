Rules & Regulations

Please read and adhere to our hostel policies carefully.

  1. Every person / student must pay an advance amount of Rs. 6,000/- while joining. Amount will not be refunded if student leaves the hostel in the middle of the year.
  2. Fee should be paid on before 5th of every month.
  3. Reduction of fee amount will not be done for any type of holidays.
  4. Students are not allowed to go out after 10 pm. Students should reach hostel before 10 pm.
  5. Students have to sign the register before leaving for home or any outing and after returning.
  6. Students should follow mess timings strictly. Taking food to room is not allowed only in case of ill health.
  7. Outsiders and friends are not allowed into the hostel, since the rooms contain belongings of other roommates too.
  8. Students are responsible to take care of their belongings (valuable items). Management is not responsible for loss of your belongings.
  9. Students are not allowed to use electronic gadgets like heaters, induction stove & iron box. If any students found using the said items the gadgets will be confiscated.
  10. TURN OFF ALL THE SWITCHES IN ROOM BEFORE YOU LEAVE.
  11. Do not wash the clothes in bathrooms & Don't write or draw on the walls. Fine will be deducted from your advance deposit.
  12. Visitors (Friend or Family) are not allowed to stay the whole day, short visits only. Rs.250/- fine per person if they stay the full day.
  13. Submission of valid Aadhaar Card is MANDATORY for all residents and daily stay guests (Daily stay available at Rs. 300/- per day).

I agree to all the rules & regulations mentioned above and will abide by them.
